About me

My journey into technology & design

Growing up in Sierra Leone, I witnessed firsthand how technology could bridge gaps and solve everyday challenges. This sparked my journey into Information Systems, design, and web development — a path that led me to found ErrandBoy Express and work with brands like Salone Padi and Divine Grace Solutions.

I believe great digital solutions don't just look good; they solve real problems. Whether I'm designing a brand identity, building a web application, or brainstorming the next startup idea, my focus is always on creating impact — for my community in Sierra Leone and beyond.

When I'm not coding or designing, you'll find me exploring AI tools, learning about entrepreneurship, or thinking about how technology can transform lives across West Africa.
